# 🎬 AI-Powered Video & Audio Transcriber

An advanced Video & YouTube Transcriber that directly converts video and audio into accurate text transcripts and timestamped SRT subtitle files. Built with **Faster-Whisper** and **Gradio** with ZeroGPU acceleration.

### Features
- 🚀 **YouTube & Direct Upload**: Input YouTube URL or upload MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WebM, MP3, WAV.
- ⚡ **Faster-Whisper + CTranslate2**: Fast, accurate speech-to-text inference.
- 📝 **Export Options**: Download full transcript as TXT or timestamped subtitle as SRT.
- 🎯 **Ground Truth Transcription**: Exact voice reproduction without AI modifications.
